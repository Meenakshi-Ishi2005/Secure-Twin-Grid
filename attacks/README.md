# Attack Vectors & Adversarial Testing

This module simulates cyber-physical and market attacks to assess the resilience of the smart microgrid.

## Planned Capabilities
- **False Data Injection Attacks (FDIA)**: Biased or scaled meter tampering.
- **Double-Spending & Replay Attacks**: Submitting duplicate transaction claims.
- **Sybil & Collusion Attacks**: Coordinated market price manipulation.
- **Denial of Service (DoS)**: High-rate synthetic transaction floods.
